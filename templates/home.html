Why This Matters

Nigeria’s technology sector is growing rapidly, yet many Muslim youths remain underrepresented in emerging high-growth fields like software development, data science, and AI.

  • Closing the Gap: Providing structured platforms for mentorship and industry exposure.
  • Ethical Technology: Integrating Islamic ethical values into conversations around emerging technologies and digital innovation.
  • Value-Driven Leadership: Empowering young Muslims to develop skills while upholding clear moral principles.

Our Vision & Objectives

IBMTS is envisioned as the seed of a long-term network for mentorship, knowledge sharing, and community-driven innovation across Nigeria and beyond.

  • Community Building: Establishing a strong, lasting network of Muslim tech professionals.
  • Innovation Catalyst: Promoting tech products and collaborative initiatives solving community challenges.
  • Economic Empowerment: Advancing career growth and unlocking opportunities within the global digital economy.
